# Original Brief

Mathis's founding brief, written 2026-09-30 at the start of the project. Kept **verbatim** as the source of truth for the original intent. Later refinements live in [decisions-log.md](decisions-log.md), and where they differ, the decisions log wins.

---

Overall style

* Ironman like - cockpit like - visionary, with nature sanctuary - Futuristic looking, dune like, dystopian like. The flame, the spirit, your soul, should be at the center of this app like the gemstone in the app Opal, it should be the central elements of what keep track of the process.
* Modern app feel, with haptic responses, nice in game effects, nice imagery, and feelings of being completed in the app. Using as much the human sense as possible to keep the user engaged.

Goal of this app

* To track your progress in life, have a life companion to fall back on and listen to in order to grow as a human being. To quantify 1% daily improvement, and grow with you throughout time, proof of your growth and consistency.
* The goal of the app is to turn you into your best self, help you unlock your potential while having fun. Should be personal but also shareable.
* Should propose tasks, actions, new things, skill learning, challenges, which will ultimately turn you into a better human. Should be long lasting goals but also daily challenges for spontaneity and freshness.

Core functionality

* Tracking process should be at the epicenter, should be satisfying, easy, and quick. This neess to become the part people fall in love with not the part which makes people leave the app.
* Helping choose for user what's good them, less reliant therefore on their motivation and more simply following what is asked of them.
* This app has for goal to do good, a lot of good, to help people feel better, grow, help others, help the earth, Help people reach their goals.
* Like Opal, the game should include milestones, which will redesign the feel of the app based on the achieved milestone, this is the gamification part. People should feel a sense of entiltelement to their account like someone would to their fortnite account because they had a skin in 2015 which others people cannot habve. There should be daily weekly or yearly challenges which will allow the user to earn a new design, icon or title, and should be nice and amazing so that in terms of the change in experience of the game it adds so that people really want those.
* The flame, your flame, your soul, should be at the epicenter of this app, like the gem in Opal it should change and grow with time. This game has for time horizons years and decades therefore should be timeless. It should follow people and be the recognition and proof of how far they have come in life.
* In the future there should be challenges which can only unlock or be completed in partnertship with other apps or brands, like run 2km with strava to actually trakc if 2km have been ran, they idea too here is that people should do these things for themselves, not for someone else, therefore the idea is that the process, the doing is what's important for them. People shouldn't even think about ticking an action without doing it because that would defeat the whole purpose of the app, and they should know that. The tracking mechanism should really be a nice and easy and super satsifying part of the app.

What this app shouldn't be

* It shouldn't be just another habit tracker, with simple mechanisms, which people only use for 1 week and then stop using it, it should include real principles which will create this sense of wanting to come back, of credibility in the app actually giving you the achievements, and making you want to grow, ideally friends should be able to share profiles, add friends, see where people are at with there development of their life on specific goals, see the icon and banners from the past which they have achieved.
* It shouldn't be linnear, everyone should have a tailored experience, to their own life, their own needs, where they currently stand at. This refining process of making it personlized to the user is at the core too of what this app should be, making it about you, making you grow, turning you into a better person, and having proof of that through this app. This is vital.
* Shouldn't be a half ass project, this is an all out project, one which has for goal to revolutionze the modern world, one which has for goal to have millions of users come and start building their life with this help.
* Shouldn't be an app that sells bullshit and scam, should really help people become better, develop confidence in themselves, challenge themselves, get rewards, and grow, become nicer, stronger, smarter, become sources of good for humanity. People shouldn't be able to find exactly what's needed for them to grow on this app, actually better, the app should know exactly what's needed for someone to grow, through their use, their frequency of use of the app, should recommend the right actions, the right habits, the right "getting out of your comfort zone" challenges.

What is the ambition for this app.

* The ambition for this app is to create something which has never been done before
* A project at scale to help people become better humans for themselves, the people around them and the planet.
* A project at scale to help people regain a sense of urgency over their life, to regain the core basics of what a good life should look like, and to feel alive again in a world of distraction
* A project that would bring millions of people together, like pokemon go did at the time, and which would prevail and stay around for decades thanks to its prfoundly good nature.
* The game should be at its center user focused, this should be all about making the user feel good, feel nice about their achievements, regain a sense of ownership of their life, and give them the approriate rewards for doing what's expected of them.
* This game should bring people together, should include partnerships with other brands to create a whole ecosystem. Not only should this be a game, but this should become a whole ecosystem, a second nature of your life, a way to externalize all the efforts you are doing in order to better yourself.

How to use AI appropriately in order to actually make this game,

* Should not be a one step process to build
* shouldn't revolve around only one AI
* Not only one iterations
* The alignement with the goal and purpose of the app in the beginning is the single most important first step, and should be the main point of focus in the beginning, ensuring the right direction is taken from the get go to facilitate further development of the app.
* A lot of thorough planning, in terms of the infrastructure, both front and back end, should go into this project before even starting to code anything, the planning process should take a long long time, should be extremely thorough, as thorough as possible in order to not leave any room for guessing.

Based on all of this I want us to have a conversation as I would with a partner to keep refining where needed the plan to actually start building this app.
