BUILD:
1. A new SwiftUI map (Shell/IslandMapView.swift) shown for `.map` instead of the old one: a vertical journey that scrolls upward, the Sanctuary at the bottom, opening scrolled so the Flame's stone sits comfortably in view. The deep night-teal to indigo sky with a slow soft swirl, faint stars, drifting map-cloud banks and a few distant map-islet rocks (parallax as you scroll). The islands zig-zag left and right, large, never cut by the screen edges. Between consecutive islands, a curved path of map-stone tiles, one per level; on each tile a round medallion with its level number (passed: warm gold; current: gold with a soft pulsing ring and the Flame standing on it; ahead: dim grey-blue stone). The Flame on its stone is the current form's painted sprite (`model.form.base + "-keyed.png"`, e.g. depth-blaze-keyed.png), small, glowing, gently breathing; tapping it makes it hop.
2. Each island's name on an elegant small nameplate on or just under it (serif `.story` font, cream text on a dark plate with a gold edge and a crisp lip, never covering the landmark; keep "The" in the names: use `RealmID.name`). States: open = bright and warm; buyable = soft glow plus a coin badge with the price and a small Buy button (dimmed if not affordable); toReach = a small flame icon and "Level N"; waiting = veiled in mist, darker, a small lock and "Level N"; sealed (the Veil) = a misty silhouette.
3. Tapping an island selects it and shows the existing card (reuse `card(_:)`, `buy(_:)` and the coin flight from LivingMapView; move or share code as you see fit). Buying keeps the existing rules and ceremony: the island wakes (its mist lifts with a warm bloom). Entering a world: replace the 3D dive with a 2D one (the view zooms toward the island, its light fills the screen), then open the world exactly as `enter(_:)` does after its dive today (same screen change, same `arrivingByDive` handling). The guided tour's map step must still work.
4. Alive, always (decision 93): islands bob very gently, each in its own rhythm; clouds drift; the current stone's ring pulses; waterfalls may shimmer if cheap. Nothing is a still picture.
5. The old RealityKit map must not keep rendering under the new one: make sure that while the map screen is shown, the single 3D view draws nothing heavy (do not build MapScene for the map screen, or pause the world with `model.worldPaused`), and that leaving the map restores home/realms exactly as before. Keep MapScene.swift in the project even if unused. Check nothing else needs `model.mapBuilt` (ShellModel.ensure / preloadCore / test flags / `-realm` travel).
6. Performance: 60 fps on an iPhone 15. No SwiftUI blur or big soft shadows over moving content (decision 165); glows are painted gradients; keep image sizes as given; use TimelineView only where something moves.
