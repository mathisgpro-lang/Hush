# Independent review: P3 phase plan

**Verdict: not build-ready against Part 9 §4.** The scope and discovery direction are coherent, and the main story thresholds match the code. Several required gates remain promises, however. One AI builder with AI-only art can plausibly develop this slice incrementally; P2 does not yet support the promise of final quality in 4–6 build weeks.

**Gate coverage:** §4.1 beats — partial; §4.2 discovery and Journey set — covered at flow/catalog level; §4.3 examples — all eight present, with unresolved rules; §4.4 safety tables — missing; §4.5 backup passed — not evidenced; §4.6 character states/ceremonies — partial; §4.7 real-engine pacing — incomplete; §4.8 accessible routes/testing — promised, not demonstrated.

Sources checked: Parts 1–9, decisions 84–103, P2 `PIPELINE.md`, the pacing script, and the existing Domain and relevant app integration code. Finding 11 separately addresses later decisions already in this checkout. BLOCKING means resolve before the affected work proceeds; DEFERRABLE means resolve in the named build plan.

1. **BLOCKING — Required safety tables are postponed.** B.5 says tables will be written before a Journey becomes usable; §4.4 requires them in the phase plan. Six Journey names and “gentle ramp from WHO guidance” leave the actual content undefined. **Fix:** attach each template's steps, limits, sources, permitted adaptations, stop/skip alternatives and referral wording. Cover Encounters, Gatherings and combined demand across Journeys. Include trusted-person/no-contact, indoor and seated alternatives (R10).

2. **BLOCKING — The backup gate is promised, not passed.** B.5 moves verification to before daily use, while §4.5 requires a passed gate. No round-trip result is linked. **Fix:** record an export/import test covering every R11 field, a tested migration and stable weekly re-signing. Explain ordinary iPhone backup and explicitly forbid deleting the real app to fix signing. If future fields prevent passing now, reconcile the gate timing explicitly rather than claim compliance.

3. **BLOCKING — Matching thresholds conceal an ownership change.** B.2 correctly matches `Journey.step` for steps 1–4, Flame at seven active days, and Metal at 30 active days plus Depth 60. But `CharacterStage.owned` uses witnessed scenes; `World.isOpen` uses witnessed keys or current eligibility; `AppModel` owns Metal only after `witnessForge`. This differs from R2/B.6's permanent grants independent of animation. **Fix:** specify separate eligibility, grant and witnessed records, migration of old keys, and restart-safe grants after undo expires. Correct the abbreviated `CharacterStage.earned` call to include `totalDepth`. Separately restrict P3 scene availability: the existing `WorldCatalog` opens other realms at Depth 12 or Metal.

4. **BLOCKING — The queue does not guarantee every Act I scene.** `Journey.step` returns the highest step, and existing `witnessUnlock` marks earlier steps witnessed too. B.6 leaves catch-up and imported records undefined; “next app open” is ambiguous. The Return lacks an exact trigger and must follow the first action back (R1), not merely opening. **Fix:** enumerate eligible unseen beats with stable IDs and prerequisites; define foreground/restart, interruption and replay behavior. Keep feature access independent of the queue. Resolve B.2's “Map”/“Keepers screen” replay destinations against B.6's “everything” in the Book.

5. **BLOCKING — The pacing report cannot establish content coverage.** `reference/pacing_simulation.py` imports two reference functions and recreates growth/stage calculations rather than running the Swift engine. “Planned rest” is an empty day. Metal, Sanctuary levels, Journey finales and map thresholds are absent. B.4 also calls day 55 reachable inside six weeks. **Fix:** run dated histories through the actual engine, including genuine planned rest, and list available content at every threshold. Cover existing/imported progress and longer Act I histories that reach Blaze. Keep six weeks illustrative; never cap or accelerate growth to fit the art.

6. **BLOCKING — P2 has not validated the production scope or estimate.** `PIPELINE.md` explicitly leaves rebuilding geometry out of Grove restoration and records incomplete depth layers, contact shading, integration and weather. Therefore “the painted route makes every level available” does not establish levels 4–5 at final quality. Device measurements remain owed under decision 100; decision 103 deferred only walking in depth. **Fix:** inventory objects, inhabitants and assets for each reachable restoration level and Sanctuary Age. Complete a representative production trial with source files, licenses, reproducible export, sound and device results. Record accepted quality compromises and re-estimate one-builder effort, art retries and Mathis's 1–2 hours/day of review.

7. **BLOCKING — Two stage names do not specify character states.** B.6 omits the per-stage state coverage required by §4.6. Part 3 §11 asks an armless Spark to pat its chest and inspect its hands; the supplied Flame rig has five tongues while Part 3 §3 specifies four. **Fix:** reconcile gestures and silhouettes, then map Spark/Flame waking, sleep, moods, touch, completion, travel, speech and ceremonies to supported motions. Include Part 3 §12's device recording “alive check” in every build plan, including synchronized babble and touch response.

8. **BLOCKING — Accessibility and quality modes lack an implementation map.** B.7 repeats the two-tap goal without routes or test evidence; B.9 names only full/reduced performance. R4 requires five modes and separate thermal, power and user controls. **Fix:** add a labeled route table and renderer-specific mode table, retaining the five-second step-down/60-second recovery rule. Record VoiceOver, largest-text and Reduce Motion checks; accessible text must appear immediately. Verify completion, undo and navigation with an animated Flame in every mode.

9. **BLOCKING — New restoration rules leave consequential guesses.** B.4 adds Sanctuary restoration from total Depth without explaining its interaction with Ages. Its catalog example retains old thresholds only until the next level, leaving subsequent migration undefined. **Fix:** define Sanctuary grant IDs, independent restoration/Vitality/Age inputs, and version rules for the complete progression. State that Sanctuary restoration creates no extra Beacon. Give exact progress behavior after corrections below a granted floor and show all four low/high restoration/Vitality combinations (R1–R2).

10. **DEFERRABLE — Assign the remaining content choices to build plans.** Discovery leaves keyword ties, sampler-day counting and sensitive unsupported requests unspecified. Quiet-pillar reminders (R16) have no assigned work; R9's reflections omit challenge fit and the explicit “felt obliged” failure signal. Sound plan 9 omits Part 7's Sanctuary/Grove themes. **Fix:** settle discovery in plan 3/6, reminders and reflections in plan 8, and themes or an approved scope change in plan 9. Apply reminder frequency limits and both dismissal choices; do not let sensitive requests fall into ordinary keyword matching.

11. **BLOCKING — Later decisions supersede this build route.** Beyond the requested decision-103 baseline, decision 104 approves P3, 108 pauses it for P2.5, 109 changes the introduction, and 111 selects Unity and a 3D world. B.1/B.8 still prescribe the painted RealityKit port. **Fix:** mark this as a historical proposal or reconcile the plan with those decisions before implementation. Preserve settled approvals, identify the new engine/record migration work, and validate the Unity route independently of the old painted-route results.
