**Verdict: the visual direction works, but the daily screen needs fixes before sign-off.** The warm palette, clear streak number and week row follow the sketches. Removing the rays was intentional and correct.

I compared all four sketches with all eight captures and the montage, and traced the current code. Ordinary streak counts, shields, two-quest repair, permanent forms, form thresholds and weekly XP/level changes look correct. A repair day's first quest leaves the streak unchanged; the second restores it and correctly triggers the lit screen. The current suite has 61 assertions, not 57; I reviewed them without rerunning the app. Phone performance remains unverified. Some captures predate fixes: the old lit-bar title and “Ready to beat it?” are already corrected in source.

Code references below are under `prototypes/p2-spikes/SpikeLab/Shell/`. Findings are ordered by impact; visual priorities are numbered within their findings.

1. **MUST-FIX — Tomorrow's welcome is missing when the app stays running overnight.** `ShellModel.swift:416–418` checks the day only at initialization; `432–438` does so through the test button. There is no foreground/day-change handler. Reopening a backgrounded Monday session on Tuesday therefore misses the welcome and pouch, and retains the old quest list. An open report can also remain dated yesterday. **Fix:** refresh the day once on foreground entry and calendar-day changes, including any visible report, while preserving birth-day/tour exclusions. [Apple's scene-phase API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui/scenephase/active) provides the foreground signal. Check both overnight reopening and staying open through midnight.

2. **MUST-FIX — Freeze the quest's numbers and original form before delaying its celebration.** `HomeView.swift:670–678` waits 300 ms before reconstructing “before” by removing the record's current last quest (`DailyWelcome.swift:12–13`). Two quick completions can produce **1 → 1 with a “+1”**, and crossing midnight during the delay can produce tomorrow's report for yesterday's quest. Separately, `HomeView.swift:688` changes `model.form` before the reveal text reads it (`DailyWelcome.swift:403`), so a real unlock still says **“Your Kindled became a Kindled.”** **Fix:** capture the completion time, before/after reports and original displayed form together when recording the quest; carry those fixed values through the animation. Test actual quest completions, not only synthetic welcome flags.

3. **MUST-FIX — Keep the form bar's scale and historical marker honest.** With best streak 10 and a restarted streak of 6, the Kindled bar is 6/14, or **43%**. Completing day 7 changes its starting point to 7, making the same target's bar **0%** (`Progression.swift:150`). Also, `DailyReport.swift:96` moves a larger historical marker down to today's smaller value: an actual historical 10/14 becomes 3/14 after a break. **Fix:** keep a stable scale while pursuing the same form. Preserve the real historical marker, or explicitly omit the comparison after a restart. Add both cases to the checks; never manufacture rightward improvement.

4. **MUST-FIX — Remove pressure and unsupported promises.** Comeback/repair always says “Let's never miss twice” (`DailyWelcome.swift:489`), and repair asks users to “save” their Flame (`DailyReport.swift:154`). The rotating messages also include “Don't break the chain,” “37 times better,” and “Hard days count double” (`StreakViews.swift:160–184`). These imply guilt, guaranteed personal growth or rewards the game does not calculate. **Fix:** review the whole quote pool. Use “Your progress is still here” and “Two quests today restore your 4-day streak. Your forms and progress stay either way.” The approved sketch's pressure language also needs this correction.

5. **MUST-FIX — Finish the rendering lifecycle and prove phone performance.** The opaque daily screen leaves the world updating underneath its second RealityView (`HomeView.swift:258`; `ShellModel.swift:355`). Its six-second warm-up flag belongs to each HomeView, so navigating back from the map or entering another realm can repeat warm-up outside launch. `waitDrawn()` also proceeds after 1.6 seconds without frames, despite documented cold delays of 3.5–5 seconds. Its counter measures [scene-update callbacks](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/realitykit/sceneevents/update), not confirmed displayed images. **Fix:** own warm-up once in the model, suspend covered world work, handle readiness/failure explicitly and retain a usable continue action. Measure cold launch, first quest, evolution and repeated navigation on the iPhone 15. This is a confirmed workload/readiness risk, not a measured frame-rate failure.

6. **MUST-FIX — Visual priority 1: stop clipping the Spark.** `verification/first.png` cuts its crown off along a straight edge. Fixed camera framing and the constrained stage do not accommodate its moving tips (`DailyWelcome.swift:58,379–393`). **Fix:** fit each form's visible bounds to the actual available space, with room above the fire. Verify all six forms, especially first/comeback screens with the latest longer copy.

7. **NICE-TO-HAVE — Visual priority 2: make supporting numbers easier to read.** Growth details can shrink from 13 to 10.4 points; badges are 11 points (`DailyWelcome.swift:263–272`). “NOW LIT” is 8.5 points and escapes the week card's bottom padding (`467–468`). **Fix:** give details a second line instead of shrinking them, and reserve a proper row for the lit label. Check at actual phone size and larger text settings.

8. **NICE-TO-HAVE — Visual priority 3: use the lit screen's empty space.** The sketch gives more space to the Flame and number; the build leaves a large empty area above a bottom-aligned Flame capped at 250 points (`DailyWelcome.swift:380–393`). **Fix:** enlarge or recenter the lit composition and separate the lower groups. After that, polish the tiny shield, flat level badge and hard-edged ground oval.

9. **NICE-TO-HAVE — Clarify that the outlined “1” is an invitation.** First/comeback shows “1” while the real streak is zero (`DailyWelcome.swift:407–410`). It matches the sketch but can suggest opening already earned a day. **Fix:** label it “DAY 1 · WAITING FOR A QUEST,” keeping the earned count at zero until the quest.
