# Plan 2.0 · Part 2 — The World Bible

*The Emberlands: their history, their places, their people, and the story you'll walk through over years.*

**Status:** draft. Names and lore are proposals (decision 63; Mathis asked Claude to invent, inspired by great universes) · **Written:** 2026-10-01
**Inspirations (spirit, never copied):** Studio Ghibli (*Spirited Away*, *Howl's Moving Castle*): warmth, spirits with personality, a world that feels lived-in. *Journey*: wordless awe, other travellers as distant lights. *Spiritfarer*: tenderness, caring for others as gameplay. *Ori*: luminous restoration of a fading land. *Zelda: Breath of the Wild*: a map revealed by exploring. *Sky: Children of the Light*: gentle community through light. *Animal Crossing*: real-time seasons, a home you shape. *Avatar: The Last Airbender*: elemental realms with distinct cultures and wise mentors.

---

## 1. The world in one breath

> **The Emberlands** were once bright: every living person carried a flame, and every flame fed the **First Hearth** at the heart of the world. Then came **the Hush**, not a monster, but a forgetting. People got lost in noise and distraction, and their flames went quiet. One by one, the realms dimmed, the creatures hid, and grey mist settled over everything.
>
> The ruins you wake in were built by the last ones who remembered. **Your Flame is new: the first to be born in a long time.** Everything you do in your real life gives it light, and with that light, the world remembers itself.

**Tone:** warm, wondrous, gently melancholic, never dark or violent. **No enemies to fight**: the only "opponent" is the Hush, and you don't defeat it, you *outshine* it. The world is healed by living, not by fighting.

## 2. Cosmology

| Concept | Meaning in the world | Mirror in real life |
|---|---|---|
| **The First Hearth** | The source of all flames, at the center of the world, now cold and silent | The deepest sense of being alive |
| **Flames** | Every person's soul, as a small living fire with a face and a heart | You |
| **Light** | What a flame gives off when it truly lives | Every real action |
| **The Hush** | A soft grey mist of forgetting that settles where no one lives fully | Distraction, numbness, autopilot |
| **The Keepers** | Ancient spirits who each guard one realm's wisdom | The knowledge you need (with real, cited sources) |
| **Beacons** | One per realm, cold for centuries; relit when that realm is fully restored. Any three open Act III; all six complete the optional **Full Circle** (decision 96) | Long-term goals achieved |
| **Embers of others** | Faint lights of other travellers seen in the distance | Community (the Gathering, later social) |

**The rule of the world:** *light can't be taken, bought or borrowed. It can only be lived.*

The Sanctuary sits at the bottom of a great valley. Each realm lies along the valley's rising slopes. The higher you go, the older and stranger the world becomes.

## 4. The Sanctuary: your home

**What it is:** the ruined temple of the last flame-keepers. Sandstone arches, a still pool, roots and vines, and at its center **your Hearth**, where your Flame was born and where it sleeps.

**How it grows** (the Ages of the Sanctuary, from chapter 03's Eras):

| Age | Unlocks by | The Sanctuary becomes |
|---|---|---|
| **Age of Stone** | Birth | Ruins, a single hearth, moss |
| **Age of Metal** | The Forge (30 active days + Depth 60) | Forged braziers, repaired arches, bronze and blue inlays |
| **Age of Crystal** | ~6 months lived | Crystal windows, light pouring through, a garden of glass flowers |
| **Age of Light** | ~2 years | Luminous architecture, the Hearth visible from every realm |
| **Beyond** | Years | Unknown |

**Your home, your way (keepsakes):** the Sanctuary has places to set keepsakes you've earned: shelves, the hearth mantle, the garden, the pool. Arrange them however you like. Each keepsake has a date and a story. Your home becomes the museum of your life.

**Its inhabitants:**
- **The Lamplighter**: an old, hooded flame whose light is very dim. They have waited centuries for a new flame to be born. Mentor of Act I, teller of lore, gentle and wry. *"Every flame that wakes makes this place a little warmer. Welcome, little one."*
- Over time, creatures from restored realms come to visit (a fox from the Grove, an owlet from the Library…).

## 5. The six realms

Each realm mirrors one pillar. Each has a **Keeper**, a **look**, a **sound**, **places**, **Journeys**, **Encounters**, a **Beacon**, and its own story.

### 5.1 The Ascent Highlands (Body)
- **Look:** terraced hills, cliffs and waterfalls, wind-carved stone stairs, an old observatory on the summit. Warm ember and sunrise tones.
- **Sound:** wind, drums of distant waterfalls, a steady heartbeat rhythm in the music.
- **Keeper: Brakka**, an ancient stone tortoise who carries a small mountain on her back. She is slow, patient and immovable, and believes strength is built step by step. *"The mountain didn't grow in a day. Neither will you. Walk."*
- **Places:**
  - **The Foot of the Stairs**: where every road begins.
  - **The Waterfall Steps**: the realm's signature sight; flowing again at level 3.
  - **The Strong Stones**: a training ground of fallen arches.
  - **The Night Garden**: where sleep is tended; moonflowers.
  - **The Summit Observatory**: the Beacon.
- **Journeys:** The Road to the First Summit (running), The Strong Stones (strength), The Night Garden (sleep).
- **Encounters:** mountain goats, a lost hiker spirit, wind sprites.
- **Its story:** the observatory once watched the First Hearth's light from above. Relighting the Beacon lets you see, for the first time, the Hearth's faint glow at the valley's center.

### 5.2 The Library of Embers (Mind)
- **Look:** a floating island of towering shelves, books drifting like birds, ink-blue light, lanterns of thought.
- **Sound:** pages turning, quill scratches, a soft piano.
- **Keeper: Quill**, an owl folded from living paper whose feathers are pages of every book ever written. Curious, playful, sometimes forgetful. *"Every question you ask writes a new page. Ask more."*
- **Places:**
  - **The Unread Shelf**: books waiting for a reader.
  - **The Reading Room**: its fireplace relit by reading.
  - **The Map Room**: a skill or language being learned shows up here as a map being drawn.
  - **The Blank Pages**: where the Book of Your Flame is kept.
  - **The Lantern of Thought**: the Beacon.
- **Journeys:** The Unread Shelf (reading), The Map Room (learning a subject or language).
- **Encounters:** bookworms who are actual worms, a forgetful archivist ghost, paper cranes.
- **Its story:** the Library holds the records of every flame that ever lived. Some pages describe the Hush's beginning.

### 5.3 The Whispering Grove (Heart)
- **Look:** a forest of ancient trees with thousands of hanging lanterns, fireflies, mossy clearings, a stone circle at its heart.
- **Sound:** whispers in the leaves, wind chimes, warm strings.
- **Keepers: Nim & Ro**, twin lantern-fireflies who never leave each other's side. They finish each other's sentences and know everyone's name. *"A lantern alone is small." "Two lanterns are a path."*
- **Places:**
  - **The Stone Circle**: where the weekly **Gathering** (the Ritual) happens; other travellers' lights appear in the trees.
  - **The Lantern Walk**: lanterns for the people you love.
  - **The Old Oak**: messages hang from its branches.
  - **The Great Lantern**: the Beacon.
- **Journeys:** Lanterns for the Ones You Love (be closer to people).
- **Encounters:** a shy fox who misses her family, travelling musicians, a heron delivering letters.
- **Its story:** the Grove is where flames used to meet. It went quiet when people stopped reaching out. Each lantern you light is someone you called.

### 5.4 The Stillwater (Spirit)
- **Look:** a vast mirror lake at the edge of night, lotus stepping stones, a sky full of slow stars, a pavilion floating at the center.
- **Sound:** almost silence, water drops, a low singing bowl.
- **Keeper: Ilo**, a silver heron who speaks only in whispers and stands on one leg for days. Wise, kind, mysterious. *"…be still… and you'll see yourself…"*
- **Places:**
  - **The Lotus Path**: stepping stones that appear one by one as you practice calm.
  - **The Gratitude Shore**: stones you've written on.
  - **The Pavilion of Breath**.
  - **The Clear Reflection**: the Beacon. The lake fully clears and shows… something.
- **Journeys:** The Clear Water (meditation and presence), The Gratitude Shore (gratitude).
- **Encounters:** a koi who asks a question and swims away, a moon moth, a tired traveller who needs quiet.
- **Its story:** the Stillwater reflects not what is, but who you are becoming. When fully clear, it shows your Flame's future form.

### 5.5 Forge Mountain (Craft)
- **Look:** a volcano-mountain hollowed into workshops, rivers of molten light, bronze machinery, anvils that ring. Iron Man's precision meets an ancient forge.
- **Sound:** hammer rhythms, bellows, metallic resonance.
- **Keeper: Orrin**, a soot-black salamander smith with spectacles, gruff and secretly proud of every apprentice. *"Every hour leaves a mark. I can see yours."*
- **Places:**
  - **The Apprentice's Bench**: deep work sessions.
  - **The Hall of Unfinished Work**: your projects take shape here.
  - **The Great Anvil**: where keepsakes are forged at the end of a Journey.
  - **The Forge Heart**: the Beacon, and the site of the **Forge ceremony** (the Age of Metal).
- **Journeys:** The Unfinished Work (deep work, a skill or craft), Money Habits (the Ledger Room: tracking and saving habits only, never investment advice).
- **Encounters:** a broken automaton who wants to be fixed, a young salamander apprentice, a merchant of strange tools.
- **Its story:** the Forge made the first vessels for flames. The Forge ceremony (step VI) is where your Sanctuary enters the Age of Metal.

### 5.6 The Wilds (World)
- **Look:** wide green lands, rivers, abandoned villages, wildflower meadows, migrating creatures, distant mountains.
- **Sound:** birds, rivers, a folk melody.
- **Keeper: Mossbeard**, a walking forest-giant spirit, slow, funny and enormous, with a village of birds living in his beard. *"Care for a place, and it cares for you back. Simple."*
- **Places:**
  - **The Empty Village**: houses that relight as you help others.
  - **The River of Return**.
  - **The Wildflower Fields**.
  - **The Far Lookout**: where other travellers' lights become visible on the horizon.
  - **The Village Fire**: the Beacon.
- **Journeys:** Roots (caring for your space and nature), Helping Hands (helping others).
- **Encounters:** a family of hedgehogs needing a home, a lonely lighthouse keeper, a stray spirit-dog.
- **Its story:** the Wilds connect the Emberlands to the outside, to other travellers. Its Far Lookout is the threshold of Act III.

## 7. The story in Acts (the long arc)

*Durations are illustrative: Acts advance only by lived progress, never by the calendar (Part 9 R5). Act III opens with any three Beacons; all six is the optional Full Circle (decision 96, Part 9 R16).*

| Act | When (lived time, never calendar) | The story | Real-life arc |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I · The Waking** | Weeks 1–6 | Your Flame is born. The Lamplighter guides you. You discover the Spark, the Grove, the Keepers, the Book, and finally the Forge. The Age of Metal begins | Building your first habits |
| **II · The Six Beacons** | Months 2–12 | A realm's Beacon relights when the realm is fully restored (finishing a Journey leaves its own landmark). **Act III opens with any three Beacons; all six is the optional Full Circle** (decision 96). The Keepers reveal fragments of the Hush's history. Your Flame evolves to Blaze | Achieving your first long-term goals |
| **III · The Grey Sea** | Year 2 | You cross the Grey Sea and meet other travellers' lights. Together, flames begin to rekindle the First Hearth | Growing *with* others; helping others grow |
| **IV · Beyond the Veil** | Years 3+ | The origin of flames, the meaning of the Hush, and what your Flame will become | A life lived fully, and passed on |

**Story delivery:** short, wordless-first scenes (images, gestures, music), with very few words, in the Keepers' voices. The Book of Your Flame keeps every scene you've seen. **Nobody is ever forced to read lore:** it's there for the curious.

## 8. Time in the world

| Rhythm | In the world |
|---|---|
| **Day and night** | Follow your real lived day (04:00 boundary): the Sanctuary at dawn when you wake, at night when you sleep |
| **Weather** | Gentle and random-seeded (rain in the Grove, wind in the Highlands, snow in Embernight) |
| **Seasons** | **Thaw** (Mar–May): flowers return. **Long Light** (Jun–Aug): festivals, long evenings, the Long Light Gathering. **Harvest** (Sep–Nov): amber light, gratitude. **Embernight** (Dec–Feb): snow, lanterns, the Embernight Gathering. Each season has a story chapter and a seasonal keepsake earned by living through it |
| **Your Flame's Birthday** | Once a year, a celebration and a new volume of the Book |

## 9. World rules (for every future realm, story and feature)

1. **Light is only earned by real living.** Nothing in-app produces light.
2. **Nothing is ever destroyed.** The Hush covers; it never erases.
3. **No enemies, no combat, no death.** Challenge comes from real life, wonder from the world.
4. **Every creature has a little story**, and helping them mirrors a real kindness.
5. **Wisdom has sources.** A Keeper's counsel always comes from real, cited knowledge (decision 18).
6. **Mystery is earned.** Mist hides what you haven't lived toward yet.
7. **The world reflects you.** Its balance, colors and inhabitants mirror your pillars.
8. **Others are lights, not rankings.** You see that others walk, never who's "ahead."
