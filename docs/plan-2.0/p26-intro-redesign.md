# The introduction, redesigned (plan, 2026-10-05)

*Source: Mathis: "extensive research on how the best apps build onboarding… tailored for this app. Keep the Flame hatching and becoming alive, a few questions to set up the account and understand where the user stands, the evolutions in shadow." Status: **draft, waiting for approval.***

## What the research says (and what it means for Hush)

| Finding | From | For Hush |
|---|---|---|
| Build the emotional bond **before** asking anything: hatch, name, a pet that reacts | Finch (45 screens; egg picker, naming, the pet reacting to each choice) | The hatching opens the app; the Spark reacts to every answer |
| The mascot talks to you and asks your name first | Duolingo ("Hi! What's your name?") | The Spark, just born, asks who you are |
| Ask the **why** before anything else | Duolingo ("Why are you learning?"), Calm ("What brings you here?") | "What brings you here?" in big cards |
| An honest **starting assessment**, compared later | Life Reset (Day 1 rating vs Day 66), Finch survey | A quick check of your six pillars = your **Day 1 rating**, shown again at Day 66 |
| **When** you'll do it (implementation intention) | Duolingo routine, Fabulous (+20% follow-through) | "When can we meet each day?" sets your daily moment |
| A visible "building your plan" moment | Finch plan card, top-grossing apps | Preparing your path, with real steps (it really loads your worlds) |
| A **starter plan** with goals already picked | Finch | Your first three quests, picked from your answers |
| The **first win inside the onboarding** | Duolingo (first lesson before signup: +20% daily users), Headspace (a first breath) | One tiny quest done together, tailored to you, with the full reward |
| Start the streak and a **small commitment** | Duolingo (Day 1, streak target: +14% Day-7 retention with the wager) | Day 1 lit, then "Keep your flame 7 days?" (a shield at day 7) |
| Ask permissions **in context, after value** | every guide | The reminder is asked last, at the moment you chose |
| Shortest path wins; don't take data before trust; no paywall | Digia, Fabulous critique | ~12 beats, under 3 minutes, no account, no paywall, "stays on your phone" |

## The new introduction (12 beats)

1. **The Hush.** The camera drifts slowly over the grey, misty Sanctuary at the real hour. *Hush.* "The world went quiet. One ember still waits." Tap anywhere.
2. **Choose your ember.** Six embers float, one per pillar: "Which light calls you?" It sets your Flame's hint of colour and your first world.
3. **Warm it.** Hold it on the stone circle: cracks spread, rising haptics and sound, then the hatch burst. The Sanctuary brightens a little.
4. **Alive.** The Spark blinks, looks around, notices you and giggles: "…Oh! Hi. You woke me. What should I call you?" (your name)
5. **The glimpse, in shadow.** "I'm small now. If we live well together, one day I'll become…" Four dark silhouettes rise one after another, edged with faint light, the last one a "?". "…only real days can tell."
6. **Why.** "What brings you here?" Big cards: feel calmer, be healthier, closer to people, learn and grow, make things, a fresh start (pick any). The Spark answers each one.
7. **Where you stand.** "How is each part of your life lately?" Six rows (Body, Mind, Heart, Spirit, Craft, World), three taps each (low, okay, good), all on one card. The Spark mirrors you, gently. "Only you see this. It stays on your phone." This is your **Day 1 rating**.
8. **Your moment.** "When can we meet each day?" (morning, midday, evening) and "How much time?" (5, 15, 30 min), on one card.
9. **Preparing your path.** Real steps tick by: reading your answers, choosing your first quests, waking your Sanctuary (the worlds load here).
10. **Your starting light.** A parchment card:
    - your six lights as little flames at their starting size, with your strongest and where we'll start;
    - your first three quests, already picked (swap any later);
    - Day 1 of 66.
11. **The first light, together.** One quest under a minute, tailored to where you start (three slow breaths, five squats, one thing you're grateful for, a glass of water). Then the full reward: the light flies to your Spark, +XP, Day 1 lit, and a lantern in the Sanctuary relights ("That's how the world wakes").
12. **A promise, then your mornings.** "Keep your flame alive for 7 days?" (a shield at day 7) → "I'm in". Then: "Shall I bring you your day each morning at 8:00?" (Allow / Not now). The camera pulls back to the whole Sanctuary, then the short tour (satchel, world).

**Never:** an account, a paywall, "how did you hear about us", data before the bond.
**What it changes in the app:** the Day 1 rating feeds Your Flame's "Day 1 → today"; your daily moment sets the reminder time; your answers choose the quests.
