# Plan 2.0 · Part 1 — The Integrated Experience

*How your real life, your challenges, your long-term goals, the world and your Flame become one thing.*

**Status:** draft for Mathis's review · **Written:** 2026-10-01 (night) · **Builds on:** decisions 1–83, chapters 01–29
**Principle above all others:** *the world only moves when you do.* Every light in this universe was earned in real life.

---

## 1. The one idea

Today, the app has separate pieces: a Flame, a day of cards, a map, a journey marker, a Companion screen. Plan 2.0 dissolves them into **one universe with one rule**:

> **Every real thing you do becomes light. Your Flame carries that light into the world, and the world comes back to life.**

- You walk for 20 minutes → your Flame gains light → it carries the light to the **Ascent Highlands** → a path that was grey becomes green; a waterfall starts flowing again.
- You call someone you love → light → the **Whispering Grove** → a lantern relights in the trees; one of its creatures returns.
- You sit in silence for five minutes → light → the **Stillwater** → the mirror lake clears a little more.

**Nothing in the world can be bought, grinded or skipped.** There's no in-app activity that gives light. The game is entirely the *picture* of your real life, and the picture is beautiful, alive and worth coming back to.

## 3. A day in the universe

### Morning: the Sanctuary wakes
- You open the app. **Your Flame wakes up with you**: it stretches, yawns, blinks, and smiles at you (its mood follows your Vitality).
- The Sanctuary's light matches your real morning (lived day, local time).
- **Today's calls** float softly near the hearth: the steps on your Journeys today ("Ascent Highlands · Day 12 of your road to 5K: a 25-minute run"). If an **Encounter** has arrived, a creature waits at the Sanctuary gate.
- The Keeper of a realm you're walking may leave a short note: *"Brakka: the hills are kinder in the morning. Go slow today."* (always sourced, always safe)

### During the day: living
- You live your life. When you've done something, you open the app, **press and hold the call**, and the tracking moment happens:
  1. the call bursts into light;
  2. your Flame **catches it** (a little hop, a happy face);
  3. it **carries the light** toward the realm (a short flight across the Sanctuary's window into that realm's direction);
  4. a soft chime in that realm's voice, a haptic heartbeat;
  5. Height ticks up.

  The whole moment takes 1.5 seconds and never blocks you.
- Opening the world map shows the light you just added: **the Highlands are a little greener than this morning.**

### Evening: at the Hearth
- When you choose **Evening at the Hearth** (or after 20:00), your Flame walks back home, sits by the fire, and **the day's light settles into the stones**: a 15-second ritual, no input. The world map updates in the background (restoration ticks forward). Your Flame says one short line about your day, and then it falls asleep.

### When you've been away
- **1 day:** nothing happens (the free day, P6).
- **3 days:** your Flame is quieter, sitting by the hearth. A thin grey mist (the Hush) appears at the edges of the Sanctuary.
- **2 weeks:** your Flame sleeps as a small ember. The Hush covers the paths, softly. **Nothing you built is lost:** every restored place is still there under the mist.
- **The Return:** the first thing you do lifts the mist from the Sanctuary in one breath. Your Flame wakes, leaps, and the Lamplighter greets you. *Returning is the most beautiful moment in the app, never a punishment.*

## 4. A week, a month, a year

| Rhythm | What happens in the universe |
|---|---|
| **Every day** | Today's calls, the tracking moment, Evening at the Hearth, maybe an Encounter |
| **Every week** | **The Gathering**: a new shared Ritual in the Whispering Grove, everyone's lights in the trees, a dated keepsake. **A new page** in the Book of Your Flame (the Chronicle) |
| **Every month** | A **Keeper's visit**: one Keeper comes to your Sanctuary to see how far you've come in their realm, with a short scene and maybe a gift. **New moon:** a Legendary Encounter becomes possible |
| **Every season** (4 a year) | **A Season of the world**: Thaw, Long Light, Harvest, Embernight. The world changes color and weather, a seasonal story chapter opens, and a seasonal keepsake can be earned by living through the season |
| **Every year** | **Your Flame's Birthday** (the anniversary of its birth): the Book of Your Flame closes a volume, a celebration in the Sanctuary, a yearly keepsake *"Year 1, 2026–2027"*. The world's story advances one Act for everyone |

## 5. Long-term goals become Journeys (quest lines)

This is how long-term goals blend into the world. **A Path becomes a Journey.**

### Anatomy of a Journey
| Part | Real life | In the world |
|---|---|---|
| **The calling** | You choose a goal ("run my first 5K") | The realm's Keeper offers you the Journey; your Flame looks at the road ahead |
| **The road** | The plan (built on a proven skeleton, chapter 07) | A visible route through the realm, drawn on its map |
| **Waypoints** | Plan milestones (week 3: first 15-minute run) | Landmarks along the road: a bridge, a shrine, a lookout |
| **Chapters** | Phases of the plan | Regions of the road with different scenery and a short story beat at each new chapter |
| **Daily steps** | Today's session | Each completed step moves your Flame forward on the road |
| **Rest days** | Planned rest | Campfires on the road: your Flame rests and the scene is cozy (rest is part of the journey) |
| **The summit** | The goal reached (you ran the 5K) | **The finale**: the summit or beacon of the realm, a ceremony, a permanent landmark named after you, a keepsake, and a Keeper scene |
| **If life happens** | Pause, reschedule | Your Flame waits at the last campfire. The road is still there |

### Journeys in the MVP library (from chapter 07, made into quests)
| Realm | Journey | Finale |
|---|---|---|
| Ascent Highlands (Body) | *The Road to the First Summit* (walk → 5K) | The Summit Lantern |
| Ascent Highlands (Body) | *The Strong Stones* (get stronger) | Lifting the Fallen Arch |
| Ascent Highlands (Body) | *The Night Garden* (sleep better) | The Moonflower bloom |
| Library of Embers (Mind) | *The Unread Shelf* (read again) | Restoring the Reading Room |
| Whispering Grove (Heart) | *Lanterns for the Ones You Love* (be closer) | The Grove's great lantern relit |
| Stillwater (Spirit) | *The Clear Water* (find calm) | The lake's reflection fully clears |
| Forge Mountain (Craft) | *The Unfinished Work* (deep work, a skill) | Your first forged keepsake |
| The Wilds (World) | *Roots* (care for your world) | A village returns to the Wilds |

### Rules
- **At most 3 Journeys at once** (decision 05-a), shown as **roads lit on the world map**.
- **A finished Journey is permanent:** its landmark stays in the world with your Flame's mark.
- **Journeys never have failure states.** They have *pauses*. The road waits.

## 6. The tracking moment, in-universe (chapter 06 evolves)

1. **Press and hold** a call: the call glows in the realm's color, and your Flame turns toward it, eyes widening.
2. **Release:** a crisp strike; the call bursts into a mote of light.
3. **Your Flame catches it:** a hop, a little cheer (a face that's different per pillar: determined for Body, curious for Mind, warm for Heart, peaceful for Spirit, focused for Craft, caring for World).
4. **It sends the light** toward the realm: an arc out of the Sanctuary window, in that realm's direction.
5. **Landing:** a heartbeat haptic, a chime in the realm's voice, Height ticks up.
6. **Full day:** your Flame does its "proud" pose; a soft chord.

**Undo** is always there (5 seconds, then "this didn't happen"), and your Flame simply catches the light back. Never drama.

## 7. Every screen, in-universe (the new information architecture)

| Screen | Purpose | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| **The Sanctuary** (home) | Your Flame, your home, today's calls, the hearth. Living and interactive (tap your Flame; it reacts) | Today |
| **The World** | The map of the Emberlands: realms, roads (your Journeys), mist, travellers' lights | World map v1 |
| **A realm** | Enter a realm: its scene, its Keeper, your Journeys' roads in it, its restoration, encounters, lore | Location scene |
| **Your Flame** | Its stage, its story, keepsakes it wears, its personality, "how it sees you" (pillar shape) | Your Story |
| **The Book of Your Flame** | Your Chronicle, volume by volume, plus lore you've found | Chronicle + journey sheet |
| **The Keepers** | Ask the Keeper of a realm anything about your Journeys, always with sources and safety | The Voice |
| **Settings** | Morning time, backup, delete everything, sound and haptics, accessibility | Settings |

**Navigation feeling:** no tabs, no menus. You *move*: from the Sanctuary, you step out of the gate into the World; you tap a realm and travel; you enter. A small, elegant "home" ember always brings you back. It should feel like walking through a place, not using an app.

## 8. How the world restores (the visual heart of progress)

Each realm has **6 restoration levels**, driven only by that pillar's **Depth** (chapter 04, never decreasing):

| Level | Depth (Rule set v1, starting values) | The realm looks like… |
|---|---|---|
| 0 · Hushed | 0 | Grey, silent, mist; only outlines |
| 1 · First light | ≥ 3 | Color returns near the entrance; the Keeper's light appears |
| 2 · Waking | ≥ 15 | Paths clear, water moves, the first creatures return |
| 3 · Alive | ≥ 50 | The realm's signature sight is restored (the Highlands waterfall, the Grove's lanterns…) |
| 4 · Flourishing | ≥ 150 | Inhabitants, music, details everywhere, the beacon visible |
| 5 · Radiant | ≥ 400 | The realm at its full glory, with secrets revealed and the Keeper's final story |

- **Within a level, restoration is continuous**: every single action makes a small, visible difference (a lantern, a flower, a stone), so progress is *visible today* (decision 14).
- **Vitality only affects the light**, not the restoration (the Hush is mist over a world that stays restored).
- **No two worlds are alike:** your world's balance mirrors your life. A Body-and-Mind person's world has radiant Highlands and a glowing Library, while the Grove is just waking. Visiting another traveller's world (later, social) is seeing the shape of their life.

## 9. Why people will come back for years (the retention design, ethically)

| Hook | How it works here | The line we never cross |
|---|---|---|
| **Visible progress** | The world restores in front of you, every day | Numbers are honest; nothing is inflated |
| **Attachment** | Your Flame is you; it greets you and cheers for you | It never guilt-trips you, never cries because you left |
| **Curiosity** | Mist over unknown realms, lore fragments, Legendary Encounters | Mystery is earned by living, never sold |
| **Ownership** | Keepsakes dated forever, a home you built | Nothing status-bearing is ever for sale (P7) |
| **Ritual** | Morning wake-up, evening Hearth, weekly Gathering, seasons, birthdays | No streak counters, no "don't break it" |
| **Belonging** | Other travellers' lights in the Grove and the Grey Sea | No leaderboards that shame |
| **Mastery** | Journeys get harder as you grow, in the flow zone | Safety limits always win over challenge |

## 10. What stays exactly as built (the foundation)

The growth engine (Depth, Vitality, Height), the permanent record, corrections, the lived day, privacy (the user holds the data), Undo, the dealer, the Journey steps, the tests. **Plan 2.0 changes the *picture*, not the truth underneath.** The world is a new layer *on top* of the engine; restoration, stages and unlocks are all calculated from the same record.
