# P3.6 · The world map, rebuilt as floating islands on your Flame's level path (decision 189)

Mathis, 2026-10-22 (with a Plants vs. Zombies 2 world-map screenshot, `docs/design/p36/refs/inspiration-floating-islands.png`): "let's try and make the world map a complete redesign, and that the worlds actually show up like this, really going from one level to the other, taking full inspiration from this screenshot with the flying islands, and the little pathways to the different levels reaching then the next worlds, which can be bought still with coins."

Asked one question: what is each stone on the path? Mathis: **your Flame's levels**. Replaces the regions plan of the same day (`p35-map-regions.md`, stopped before any change).

## The design (what the map becomes)
- **Each world is a floating island** in a deep, softly swirling night-teal sky (the Hush), with its landmark on top and rock and roots hanging below: Sanctuary (stone circle, waterfalls, wisteria), Whispering Grove (the giant lantern tree), Stillwater (the lake, reeds, a pavilion), Ascent Highlands (peaks, windmill, falls), Library of Embers (the domed library, floating books), the Wilds (jungle, ruins), Forge Mountain (the volcanic forge), and the Veil beyond, a sealed misty silhouette.
- **A path of small floating stepping stones** links one island to the next. **Each stone is one level of your Flame** with its number on a small medallion: passed levels glow gold, **your Flame stands on its current level**, the levels ahead are dim stone.
- **The islands sit on the path at levels:** Sanctuary 1, Grove 2, Stillwater 4, Highlands 6, Library 8, Wilds 10, Forge 12 (the Veil later). Portrait phone: the map scrolls upward from the Sanctuary at the bottom.
- **Buying a world:** when your Flame reaches the next island's level, that island wakes (a glow, its price on a coin badge) and you buy it with Embers, one after another as today. Islands further ahead are misty, with a lock and the level that reaches them. (Mathis's beta still opens every world for testing; `-locked` shows the real flow.)
- Unchanged: tapping an open island enters its world (the existing card and the dive), the Ember counter, Home, the game's language (serif titles, champagne / gold, crisp lips, no big blurs), the game-wide saturation (decision 187), 60 fps on the iPhone 15.

## Who does what
- **Codex:** the sketches, then (after Mathis's OK) the art (the seven islands, the sky, the stones, badges) and its integration in the map.
- **Claude:** the rule (`RealmID.islandLevel`; a world is buyable once your Flame's level reaches its island; checks), the briefs, simulator checks, the install.
