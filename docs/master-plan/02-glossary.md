# 02 — Glossary

**Status:** ✅ Approved by Mathis on 2026-09-30 (decision 27) · **Last updated:** 2026-09-30

The official vocabulary of the project. Every document, every line of code and every AI agent uses these words with exactly these meanings. When a term changes, it changes here first.

**Status key:**
- **Settled:** decided and logged.
- **Proposed:** Claude's suggestion, pending approval.
- **Name TBD:** the concept is settled, but the in-app name isn't final.

> Names that users see in the app (like "the Spark") may still change during chapter 12 (visual and sound identity). The *concepts* here are what matter for planning.

---

## Core

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|------|---------|--------|
| **The Flame** | The user's soul made visible; the center of the app. It changes in **depth and complexity, never size**. Its state drives the look of the whole app. | Settled (7, 11) |
| **Depth** | The Flame's **permanent** layer: an accumulated record of who you've become. It only grows and can never be lost. | Settled (12) |
| **Vitality** | The Flame's **recent** layer: how you've been living lately. It dims when you're away, never dies, and relights quickly when you return. | Settled (12) |
| **Pillar** | One of six universal dimensions of life that all growth maps to. Pillars are likely the Flame's visual anatomy, each contributing its own element/layer. | Settled (13); visual role approved (27) |
| **The Companion** | The AI that knows the user, gives the knowledge, builds plans and cites its sources. **Never "coach."** | Settled (17, 18) |

## The daily loop

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Path** | A long arc toward a goal: **goal + generated plan + adaptive schedule**. Designed with the Companion, it sits inside one or more pillars and adapts as you progress or miss sessions. Example: *Sub-4 marathon*. | Settled (16, 17) |
| **Plan** | The concrete program inside a Path (a training block, a night routine, a nutrition approach), built by the Companion within expert frameworks and safety limits. | Settled (17, 18) |
| **The Spark** | One surprise challenge a day, matched to you, designed to push you out of your comfort zone. | Settled (16) |
| **Spark swap** | Swapping a Spark you don't want. **Once a day.** | Settled (27) |
| **The Ritual** | The shared daily/weekly challenge everyone does at the same time, creating belonging (the Wordle principle, rebuilt for this app). | Settled (6, 9) |
| **Action** | The single unit of doing: one thing you complete (a run, a chapter read, a call to a friend). Paths, Sparks and Rituals are all made of Actions. | Settled (27) |
| **Your day** | The set of Actions waiting for you when you open the app, drawn from your Paths, your Spark and the Ritual. | Settled (16) |

## Tracking and proof

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Tracking moment** | The instant you complete an Action. It **is** the reward: the Flame reacts, and you feel progress through haptics, sound and light. | Settled (14) |
| **Self-reported** | An Action you mark as done yourself. It counts toward your private Flame. | Settled (3) |
| **Verified** | An Action proven done (for example through Apple Health, Strava, a photo or a friend's confirmation). Required for anything shareable or status-bearing. | Settled (3); methods in chapter 09 |
| **Evidence** | Real data behind growth, pillar by pillar: measured numbers where possible (km run, sleep hours), a record of what you did where not. | Settled (15) |
| **Growth number** | The headline figure that compounds with earned daily progress, showing your "1%." It's earned, not handed out. | Settled (15); **Name TBD** |
| **Difficulty matching** | The engine that sets each Action's difficulty to *your* level, so that 1% always means real effort for you. | Settled (15) |
| **The record** | The permanent, append-only history of every Action you've ever taken (never edited, never deleted). Depth, evidence and proof are all built from it. | Settled (27); designed in Phase B |

## Rewards and milestones

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Milestone** | A major point of real progress that triggers a **Transformation**. | Settled (10) |
| **Transformation** | The big-tier reward: the Flame and the whole app's look (color, material, light, sound, haptics) change. It's rare and reserved for big milestones. | Settled (10); **Name TBD** |
| **Collectibles** | The small-tier rewards (icons, titles, banners) earned through challenges. They personalize without restyling the app. | Settled (10); **Name TBD** |
| **Time-stamped reward** | A reward that exists only for people who earned it in a specific window (for example *Week 40, 2026*), scarce because of effort and time. | Settled (6) |

## Trust

| Term | Meaning | Status |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Source attribution** | Every piece of guidance shows where it comes from. It's built into the architecture from day one. | Settled (18, 19) |
| **Expert framework** | Rules and principles defined by credible sources (training progression limits, sleep science, nutrition guidelines) that the Companion's plans must stay within. | Settled (18) |
| **Safety limit** | A hard rule the Companion cannot break (for example a maximum weekly increase in running volume). | Settled (18) |
| **Professional referral** | In sensitive areas (medical, eating, mental health), the Companion points to real professionals instead of advising. | Settled (18) |
