# Codex brief: Stone Era vessel as production-style layered assets (Slice 1)

Use your image generation tool. Style reference: docs/design/birth/04-dawn.png and docs/design/flame-concepts/B-evolution-strip.png (the leftmost, carved desert stone vessel). Read docs/master-plan/03-the-flame.md (A.1–A.4).

Create game-ready layers for the Stone Era vessel, all at the SAME framing (portrait 1024x1536, vessel centered, vessel height about 80% of the canvas, front view, identical silhouette in every layer), each on a PURE BLACK background (#000000) so they can be composited with additive/screen blending or keyed:
1. assets/flame/stone/vessel-body.png — the carved sandstone vessel (a pointed lantern-gem shape with an open central cavity), lit warmly from inside, with an EMPTY dark cavity (no fire), realistic stone texture, chisel marks, small cracks.
2. assets/flame/stone/vessel-rim-light.png — ONLY the warm inner rim light and glow the fire casts on the stone edges of the cavity (everything else black), to be blended over the body.
3. assets/flame/stone/fire-core.png — ONLY a tall, living fire shape that fits exactly inside the cavity (gold-white core, orange edges, soft tongues), black elsewhere.
4. assets/flame/stone/ember-core.png — ONLY a small glowing ember bed at the bottom of the cavity (deep red-orange), black elsewhere.
5. assets/flame/stone/veins-body.png — ONLY thin glowing ember-orange veins running up the vessel's surface from the base (as if carved lines filled with fire), black elsewhere.
6. assets/flame/stone/veins-mind.png — the same idea with thin clear-blue crystal lattice lines on the vessel surface, black elsewhere.
After saving, list files with one sentence each. Modify nothing else.
